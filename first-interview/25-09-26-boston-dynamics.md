## What this screen actually is

A People Ops screen is not a technical deep-dive. Kacey is checking fit, motivation, and logistics — not grilling on bundle adjustment. Aim for a 2-3 sentence version of each answer, then stop and let her steer.

- Have a 60-second story ready: PhD at DLR → Research Scientist at QUT → why Atlas now.
- Expect: background walkthrough, why this role, why now, availability, comp range, visa/relocation.
- Keep technical language light; save depth for the engineering interviews later in the loop.

## Plan
Saturday: VIO depth (full day)

- Morning: IMU preintegration from Forster et al., derived on paper. Noise propagation, bias Jacobians, why it is linearization-independent.
- Midday: VIO initialization and observability. Gravity, scale, velocity, bias. The four unobservable directions, degenerate motions, FEJ and consistency.
- Afternoon: filter versus smoother. MSCKF, OKVIS, VINS-Mono, ORB-SLAM3 VI. Marginalization and Schur complement. Decide what you would pick for an embedded humanoid and why.
- Evening: camera-IMU calibration, time offset, rolling shutter, online versus Kalibr. Tie to your phone and multicopter experience.

Sunday: embedded, learned methods, and application (full day)

- Morning: embedded deployment. Pull concrete numbers from AllFeature-VSLAM and the TensorRT LightGlue path. Frame budget, feature count versus accuracy, ONNX and quantization lessons, async front-end and back-end.
- Midday: learned plus geometric. Your position on learned features versus ORB on edge hardware, depth priors, DROID and DPVO against classical VIO, MASt3R and VGGT. Rehearse the ground-truth-free tuning pitch as your answer to "how do you improve on real robot data without mocap".
- Afternoon: the design sketch. Build state estimation for a humanoid in a home. Sensors, head motion and vibration, boot-time init, dynamic people, long-term maps, failure recovery, evaluation. Talk it through out loud in 10 minutes.
- Evening: breadth refresh, one hour only. Epipolar geometry, PnP, BA, loop closure, place recognition, map representations.

Monday: rehearsal and rest (half day, then stop)

- Morning: mock interview. Answer five depth questions out loud with pen and paper, timed. Then two STAR stories, VSLAM-LAB for Ownership and a failure story for Learn and Be Curious, each under three minutes.
- Midday: one Python warm-up. Write a reprojection residual and a Gauss-Newton step from scratch while narrating.
- Afternoon: test Zoom or Chime, headset, camera, and lighting. Lay out CV, pen, paper, water, and your two questions for them on Sprout's sensor and compute stack.
- Stop by 4 PM. No new material after that. Bed by 9 PM.
